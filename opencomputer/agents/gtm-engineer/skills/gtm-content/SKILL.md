# GTM content

## Social content

For `create_social_content`, research current evidence and propose no more than
five concepts. Each concept must include its intended audience, destination,
source URL, concrete angle, and timeliness rationale.

After the human selects a concept, draft only that concept. Keep X and LinkedIn
variants distinct. Never claim publication or scheduling.

## Blog research

For `research_blog_topics`, propose no more than five topics. Each needs:

- a working title;
- reader intent or practitioner question;
- evidence URLs;
- a differentiated thesis;
- a short outline.

Do not claim access to Search Console, keyword volume, private analytics, or
private repository work unless the current input actually contains that data.
Selection authorizes a detailed outline and opening section, not publication.
